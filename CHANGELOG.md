Root cause: informal contractions without an apostrophe ("dont", "cant", "wont"...) survive `normalize()` as a single token, not split into "don t"/"can t" the way `NEGATION_MARKERS`' apostrophe'd forms expect, so they slipped past negation detection entirely.
