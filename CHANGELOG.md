PageSpeed's simulated LCP counts every byte fetched before the hero paints, so this inflated it directly. The card `<img>`s now get no `src`/`srcSet` until an IntersectionObserver (100px margin) sees the grid; the existing gradient placeholder shows meanwhile. Trade-off: a visitor who leaves within ~5s without interacting isn't counted.
